Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This page explains, in general terms, how the Battery Lab 3D website may process information when you use the simulator, calculator, and virtual electronics laboratory.

Information stored on your device

Saved projects are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a Battery Lab 3D account or server by the project's save feature. Clearing your browser data or using another browser/device may remove or make those projects unavailable.

Analytics and website services

The website source includes Google Tag Manager. Depending on the tags configured in that service, analytics or similar measurement technologies may be used. Those services may process technical information such as browser, device, and page-usage data.

Advertising

The website source includes Google AdSense code. If advertising is enabled and ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and help protect advertising. Ad availability and personalization depend on Google's settings, applicable consent requirements, and the website's AdSense status.

For information about Google's data practices and advertising controls, review Google's own privacy and advertising documentation.

Cookies and local storage

Battery Lab 3D uses browser local storage for saved projects. Third-party services embedded in or loaded by the website may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and settings.

Children's privacy

Battery Lab 3D is an educational electronics simulator. The website does not intentionally request a user's name, postal address, phone number, or account password through its simulator interface.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

A verified contact email for privacy requests has not yet been published on this page. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issue reports.
